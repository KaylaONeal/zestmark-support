Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save recipes from websites or social videos?
Tap Smart Import in the toolbar, then paste a recipe webpage URL or copy/paste the video caption or transcript text. Everything is parsed directly on your device.

How do I import from Paprika?
Open the actions menu → Import Paprika or JSON (Free) and choose your .paprikarecipes, .paprikarecipe, or JSON file.

How do I export my recipes?
Open any recipe and tap Share to export Markdown or JSON for free, or choose Export All from the actions menu.

How do I restore Zestmark Pro on another device?
Open the Zestmark Pro screen (or Settings on iOS) and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account.
