Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Zestmark: Recipe Saver is designed to work locally on your device without tracking you or collecting personal information.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information, recipes, grocery lists, or reading/cooking activity.
  • Zestmark does not require user accounts, logins, third-party analytics SDKs, or advertising trackers.

2. Local Storage & Optional Webpage Fetching

  • All recipes, ingredients, notes, and grocery lists are stored locally inside your device's app sandbox.
  • When you ask Zestmark to fetch a recipe webpage URL, your device downloads that page directly from the website you entered and parses the recipe locally. Our servers never see your URLs or recipes.

3. Purchases

Zestmark Pro is processed securely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never receive your payment card or billing details.
